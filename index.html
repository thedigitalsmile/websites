05 / Objections

Honest answers,
up front.

Isn't "AI SEO" just rebranded SEO?

No. Traditional SEO optimizes pages for blue‑link rankings. AI SEO optimizes for being cited inside generated answers — different signals, different infrastructure, and different metrics. We do both, but the engine is built for the AI layer first.

We've been burned by agencies before.

Fair. Our reporting is built around the things you actually care about: inquiries, consultations booked, treatment accepted, and production. If those numbers don't move, we don't have a job.

What if AI search changes again?

It will. We build around the structural fundamentals — entity authority, structured sourcing, treatment coverage — that all major LLMs rely on. The surface UI changes; the underlying signals don't.

Will our team need to do more work?

Minimal. We need a short monthly review with the practice lead, a few case anonymized photos, and your front‑desk intake data. Everything else — strategy, content, structure, reporting — is on us.
